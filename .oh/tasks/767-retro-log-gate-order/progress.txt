## Arbitration — the flag surface

The architect and the pm proposed incompatible CLI surfaces. Decisions:

- **`--result` gains exactly one value, `GATE-PENDING`.** The pm's fifth value
  `RESOLVED` was rejected: the resolving entry's run outcome genuinely *is* `OP`,
  a fifth enum value would break any consumer keying on `OP`, and `--resolves`
  already says everything `RESOLVED` would have said.
- **Promotion fields carry the literal `pending`** (pm's shape), not the
  architect's `--candidates N` with `--memory`/`--identity` forbidden. The issue
  itself sketches "an explicit `pending` state **for the promotion fields**", and
  keeping both flags always-required avoids conditional required-argument logic.
  `--candidates` was dropped as surface with no consumer.
- **The resolution marker is `--resolves HH:MM`** (architect's `--supersedes`
  renamed): "supersedes" implies the earlier entry was wrong, and a `pending`
  entry never was.
