Learnings.
- The plan's claim that teardown is inherited from the boot smoke's trap was wrong: the
  smoke tears down on SUCCESS too, so the guard neutralises BOOT_SMOKE_DOWN_ARGS and owns
  teardown itself.
- A bare `docker inspect` re-exposes Config.Env and is rejected by a repository guard;
  narrow --format templates are both safer and shorter.
- `oh harness list --json` exposes `enabled` (computed from oh.json) but not `harnessKey`,
  so the persist half is asserted through the CLI's own view rather than by duplicating
  its id-to-field mapping in shell.
- oh-shipped-repo-overridable caught a real new regression: the hardcoded default image
  ref. Fixed with the ${OH_DEFAULT_SANDBOX_IMAGE:-...} form.
- The planner predicted sandbox-dependency-installs would need an update; the diff says
  NOT-AFFECTED. That is the step-6 gate doing its job.
