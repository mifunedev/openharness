- Used that door to reproduce the operator's reported failure in a child booted from

  created in the `home` stage, build-time `pnpm setup --force` dropped), and a failed
  provision now writes a marker that the healthcheck fails on, so a container stops
  reporting healthy with a requested tool absent.

  under PNPM_HOME, agent-browser 0.8.5, catalog installed:true. Healthcheck marker
